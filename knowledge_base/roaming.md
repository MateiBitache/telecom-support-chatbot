# Roaming

## Does my plan work in the EU?
Yes. With Roam Like at Home, you can use your included minutes, SMS and data in all EU and EEA countries at no extra cost, subject to the fair use policy.

## How do I activate roaming?
Roaming is active by default on all postpaid plans. Prepaid customers can enable it in the app under Services > Roaming. Make sure data roaming is also enabled in your phone settings.

## How much does roaming outside the EU cost?
Outside the EU, roaming is charged per day with a Travel Pass. Zone 1 countries (USA, Canada, UK, Switzerland) cost 5 EUR per day for 1 GB of data and 100 minutes. Zone 2 countries (rest of the world) cost 10 EUR per day for 500 MB of data and 50 minutes.

## My phone has no signal abroad
Restart your phone, check that data roaming is enabled, and try selecting a network manually in your phone's mobile network settings. If it still does not work, contact support so we can check your roaming status.
