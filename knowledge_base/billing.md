# Billing and Payments

## How do I pay my bill?
You can pay your monthly bill by card in the NetWave mobile app, by direct debit from your bank account, or in any NetWave store. Payments made in the app are processed instantly; bank transfers can take up to 2 business days.

## When is my bill issued?
Bills are issued on the 1st of every month and must be paid within 15 days. You receive an email and an in-app notification when a new bill is available.

## Why is my bill higher than usual?
A higher bill is usually caused by roaming outside the EU, calls to premium-rate numbers, extra mobile data bought after your included data ran out, or a first bill that includes a partial month. You can see a detailed breakdown of every charge in the app under Billing > Bill details.

## What happens if I pay late?
If your bill is not paid within 15 days, you receive a reminder. After 30 days, outgoing calls and mobile data are suspended until the payment is made. Services are restored automatically within 1 hour of payment.

## How do I set up direct debit?
Open the NetWave app, go to Billing > Payment methods > Direct debit and enter your IBAN. Direct debit becomes active from the next bill.
