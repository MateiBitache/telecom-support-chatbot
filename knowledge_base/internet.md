# Mobile Internet and Connectivity

## My mobile data is not working
First, check that mobile data is turned on and airplane mode is off. Then restart your phone. If it still does not work, check in the app that you have not used up your included data. Finally, make sure your APN is set to "internet.netwave" in your phone's mobile network settings.

## My internet is very slow
Slow internet can be caused by weak signal indoors, network congestion at busy times, or reaching your plan's fair use limit, after which speed is reduced to 1 Mbps. Try moving closer to a window, switching between 4G and 5G, or restarting your phone.

## Do I have 5G?
5G is available in all major cities for customers on Plus and Unlimited plans with a 5G-compatible phone. You can check 5G coverage for your address on the coverage map in the app.

## How do I use my phone as a hotspot?
Hotspot (tethering) is included in all plans and uses your normal mobile data. Enable it in your phone's settings under Personal Hotspot or Mobile Hotspot.

## How do I check how much data I have left?
Open the NetWave app and look at the Usage section on the home screen, or send an SMS with the word DATA to 1234 for free.
