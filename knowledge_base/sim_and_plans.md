# SIM Cards, eSIM and Plans

## How do I activate an eSIM?
Open the NetWave app, go to Services > eSIM and request an eSIM. You will receive a QR code by email. On your phone, open Settings > Mobile network > Add eSIM and scan the QR code. Activation takes up to 15 minutes.

## I lost my SIM card or my phone was stolen
Block your SIM immediately in the app under Services > Block SIM, or call 1234 from another phone. You can get a replacement SIM in any NetWave store with your ID card, keeping the same phone number.

## What is my PUK code?
Your PUK code is printed on the plastic card your SIM came in. You can also find it in the app under Services > SIM details. After 10 wrong PUK attempts, the SIM is permanently blocked and must be replaced.

## What plans do you offer?
NetWave offers three postpaid plans: Start (10 GB, 500 minutes, 9 EUR per month), Plus (50 GB, unlimited minutes, 5G, 15 EUR per month) and Unlimited (unlimited data and minutes, 5G, 25 EUR per month).

## How do I change my plan?
You can upgrade your plan at any time in the app under My plan > Change plan; the new plan starts immediately. Downgrades take effect from the next billing cycle.

## How do I keep my number when I switch to NetWave?
Number porting is free. Request it in any NetWave store or online, and your number is transferred within 3 business days. Your old contract must not have any unpaid bills.
